Airwolf tester preview: native HackRF acquisition, tunable spectrum, packet and byte inspection, explicit CRC results, and a bundled native decoder. No external Wireshark process or WebAssembly runtime is required.

This update expands native reception and preserves the context needed to inspect and replay selected frames:

- BLE legacy/extended/decision advertising, experimental AuxPtr following, connection following with verified symmetric PHY updates, CTE sample coverage, and one periodic advertising train acquired through SyncInfo/CSA #2. Captured direction and timing constrain reassembly; encrypted data remains opaque.
- Wi-Fi DSSS/CCK at 1–11 Mb/s on supported 2.4 GHz channels, with an equalized retry that recovers more CCK frames under multipath, alongside non-HT OFDM at 6–54 Mb/s on supported 2.4/5 GHz channels. Single-stream HT20 adds MCS 0–7, BCC/LDPC, mixed/greenfield, STBC and A-MPDU extraction. Recovery is bounded by received airtime and verified members retain original FCS.
- LTE MIB and scheduled system information through native SI-RNTI/PDSCH reception, ordinary P-RNTI paging and RRC inspection. Up to three cells in view are followed at once, one per PSS identity, each with its own MIBs and system information. Cell publication requires two independent PBCH observations. TDD paging in special subframes follows SIB1's special subframe pattern, including the Release 11 pattern 9. Decoded blocks report the cell's reference-signal SNR, RSRP (dBFS) and RSRQ, and Discovery lists each cell's best SNR with that block's RSRP and RSRQ. The UI reports when the sampled bandwidth is insufficient for system information.
- Experimental 5G NR SS/PBCH block search at 16/20 MS/s, 400 MHz–6 GHz: the physical cell ID from PSS/SSS and the MIB from a PBCH whose CRC passed, published once a second block confirms the cell's frame timing. MIB rows have native NR RRC inspection, PCAP export as `nr-rrc.bcch.bch`, and exact replay from saved IQ. Discovery plans NR tunings across emissions in NR bands and lists cells by SSB frequency and PCI. A real n1 cell (DSS) was received live and in Discovery when tuned to its carrier. 30 kHz SSBs are tested with generated signals only. SIB1 and paging are not implemented. An opt-in **Search 5G bands below the noise** adds NR tunings across the region's FDD NR bands, because quiet NR cells add no measurable sweep energy. On real air one run found an n28 cell (PCI 98) and the n1 cell, neither of which energy-led Discovery found.
- Corrected enhanced 802.15.4 headers, short ACKs, Zigbee application values and units. User-supplied Zigbee network/link keys now enable authenticated NWK/APS decryption; OS-protected storage falls back explicitly to session-only when secure persistence is unavailable. Native EnOcean inspection includes teach-in context and supported equipment profiles; ERP2 reception compensates frequency error.
- Experimental native LoRa CSS SF7–12, 125/250/500 kHz, explicit headers (including empty payloads with a correct PHY CRC) and a LoRaWAN Class B beacon mode (implicit header, SF8–12, beacon CRCs checked; generated signals only, no real gateway beacon yet), PHY CRC and version-aware LoRaWAN inspection. User-supplied LoRaWAN 1.0.x session and root keys verify MICs, decrypt data frames and decrypt join accepts, in the same protected key storage as Zigbee keys. Public (0x34), private (0x12) and Meshtastic (0x2B) sync words; Meshtastic payloads are encrypted and stay raw. LoRa PCAP exports use LoRaTap v1, which Wireshark dissects on its own (public-sync payloads go to its LoRaWAN dissector); an extension Wireshark skips keeps the received PHY CRC, header validity and LoRaWAN version for re-import. A bundled Wireshark plugin (`wireshark/airwolf.lua`) dissects EnOcean exports and LoRa files from earlier versions.
- Experimental receive-only CEN DSRC (EN 12253 / EN 12795) at 5.8 GHz: downlink ASK/FM0 500 kb/s and uplink backscatter 2-PSK 250 kb/s on 1.5/2.0 MHz subcarriers, FCS-checked HDLC frames with a tentative LID/MAC/LLC view. **All channels** (the default) decodes channels 1–4 at once from 20 MS/s at 5805 MHz through one shared FFT channelizer; weak uplink frames that miss the FCS by one weak bit decision or an adjacent pair are repaired and marked as such (a clean copy from another timing lane is preferred); a single channel works at 10/16/20 MS/s. Downlink verified live at AutoPASS gantries (288 frames on channels 1 and 2, every FCS valid); live uplink unverified. Application (ISO 14906) and security fields are not decoded. No PCAP export; save SigMF instead.
- Experimental native Nordic Enhanced ShockBurst (dynamic payload) at 250 kb/s, 1 Mb/s and 2 Mb/s on channels 0–125: promiscuous CRC-16 reception with two-frame address confirmation, or a configured 3–5 byte address with CRC-16/CRC-8. Bad-CRC frames are kept for known addresses, single-bit CRC-16 corrections are labelled, and the inspector rechecks the CRC. Validated with generated signals only; over-the-air reception is unverified. No PCAP export (no link type); save SigMF instead.
- Experimental **Custom** formats for radios that follow no published standard (for example CC1101/CC2500, Si4x6x, SX127x FSK and nRF raw mode): 2-FSK/GFSK with a user-defined preamble, sync word, length field, whitening and CRC, or O-QPSK at 250 kb/s with a user-defined SFD on 802.15.4-style chips. Chip presets load common defaults. Rows are labelled Custom and the inspector warns that field boundaries come from the user's settings. Custom frames replay from saved IQ with the format they were received with. Validated with generated signals; over-the-air reception of custom formats is unverified.
- GPS L1 C/A: one continuous observation with code, bit and word synchronization, decoded navigation subframes and per-word parity in the inspector.
- **Show non-conformant frames** (BLE, 802.15.4 and Zigbee; off by default) also shows frames whose checksum is correct but whose header breaks the standard, marked as such.

The IQ timeline retains original samples, groups observed frames into lanes, and links selections to fresh native replay, fields and bytes. Repeated LTE broadcasts match the physical occurrence as well as payload bytes. If a selection does not recover its chosen frame, the inspector does not silently substitute another result. SigMF exports preserve original samples, hashes and bounded receiver context.

Spectrum offers Peak and Mean detectors, channel-centered and full-capture views, channel-width guides, and native SIMD FFT/filter processing. Packet display is virtualized and batched; how many live frames are kept (1,000–20,000, default 2,000) is a remembered setting in the packet list footer. Captures open and save as classic PCAP or PCAPNG; Wi-Fi is saved with a radiotap header that keeps rate, PHY, HT parameters, channel and RSSI, 802.15.4/Zigbee with a TAP header that keeps the channel, and LoRa as LoRaTap; a mixed-protocol export, and **Capture to file** during live reception, write one PCAPNG file with an interface per link type. Multiple HackRF boards can be selected by serial; independent app instances can use separate boards without synchronized capture. Experimental Pluto+ reception over USB or Ethernet (libiio) supports spectrum, Discovery and full-precision signed-16 IQ; physical Pluto+ reception and sustained transport rates are not yet verified, and timed BLE following remains HackRF-only.

**Discovery** (formerly Forensics) finds the signals around you in one step, on HackRF and Pluto: it sweeps the bands (HackRF hardware sweep; Pluto stepped sweep), has the decoders check what it finds, always listens for BLE advertising and GPS, and lists what was confirmed by protocol: LTE cells, Wi-Fi networks, BLE advertisers and GPS satellites. LTE cells are named by the network in their SIB1, LTE candidates follow the band plan region, and a run kept as **baseline** makes later runs report new, gone and stronger signals and energy. The raw energy map, heat map and band monitor remain under **All detected energy**.

Receive-only macOS checks recovered real BLE advertising, Wi-Fi OFDM, 1 Mb/s DSSS and HT20 MCS 3, and LTE MIB/SIB1/2/3/4/7 and paging. Selected IQ passed native replay and field inspection. Generated fixtures, independent signal generators, public recordings, sanitizer checks and desktop tests provide additional coverage. Live Zigbee was received on a real Zigbee PRO network (channel 25): independently FCS-valid frames in the native worker and a selected frame inspected in the desktop app. Local sweeps did not establish live EnOcean, LoRa or CCK reception, and live GPS is unverified because no satellite reached the test antenna. See `docs/native-validation.md` for evidence and limits.

Windows x64 `.exe`, Debian x64 `.deb`, and macOS Apple Silicon/Intel `.dmg` builds are configured in GitHub Actions. macOS targets 15+; Debian targets Debian 12+ and Ubuntu 24.04+. Windows/macOS radio libraries are bundled; Linux dependencies are installed through apt. These previews are unsigned and macOS builds are not notarized. Follow `USER_GUIDE.md` for USB setup and receiver settings; release installers include SHA-256 checksums.

This is not complete standards support. BLE PAwR, asymmetric PHY changes and seamless connection updates, Wi-Fi HT40, multiple spatial streams, VHT/HE/EHT, complete LTE scheduling/user traffic, decryption outside Zigbee and LoRaWAN 1.0.x, LoRaWAN 1.1 keys and session-key derivation from joins, LoRa SF5/6/implicit headers/FSK/LR-FHSS and continuous rolling IQ remain outstanding. Planned catalog standards do not enable receivers. CI has no HackRF attached; live Windows/Linux interoperability still requires hardware testing.
